About BootArchive

BootArchive is an interactive digital museum dedicated to the visual evolution of operating system startup experiences. From the flickering command-line interfaces of the 1970s to the cinematic, high-fidelity boot sequences of today, this archive preserves the 'first impression' of computing history through educational simulations and original recreations.

Historical data is presented as educational recreations and simulations. All designs are original works inspired by era-specific computing aesthetics.
